# Start your marketing team

Install and explore without business details or credentials. Add any product, service, SaaS or agency inside the platform when you are ready. The market is an input, not a hard-coded restriction.

## Try the complete preparation flow without accounts

1. Start AI-MOS and create your workspace account.
2. On Overview, choose **Fill with a fictional sample**.
3. Choose **Start campaign preparation**. A single server operation creates the sample business/contact/campaign and starts durable preparation. Retrying the same brief uses the same request identity.
4. Open **Operations / Plans & tasks** to follow progress. The team prepares research, strategy, content, an outreach draft and exact-copy approvals.
5. Review the prepared work in **Approvals**. Choose sandbox execution to simulate a send. No real email is transmitted.
6. Explore Inbox, Meetings, Revenue and Customers using the labelled sandbox actions. A simulated signed payment receipt drives customer onboarding. Test and live outcomes remain separate.

Sample businesses use invalid-domain recipients and are marked sandbox-only. Their AI calls remain simulated even if a model account is connected. Live sending, bookings and checkout creation are blocked for them. Replace sample material with your own reviewed facts in a separate real business before live use.

## Start with your own business

On Overview enter your business name, a concise approved offer, intended customers and campaign goal. Keep the offer under 80 words so grounded outreach can stay within its word limit. You can add detailed documents, pricing and policies later in Brand Brain. No JSON, code or developer handoff is required.

Connect the services you need in Integrations. Placeholder settings stay disabled. Each service has its own verification and enable step. An enabled model can incur usage charges within the configured budget; a search connection can incur its provider's charges. Use Usage & cost to set caps before connecting paid services.

Enable search in the brief to research the supplied audience. Current connected discovery uses Brave Search and bounded HTTPS reads of observed business pages; CSV/first-party imports are also supported. It cannot access every internet source or bypass login/paywalls. It never guesses email addresses. Observed contacts are scored and drafted automatically, then remain blocked from sending until contactability evidence and the exact output are reviewed.

For an existing business, open **Operations / Command Center / Autopilot** rather than creating another business with the same name. Add selected contacts, objective and optional research. The platform does not require a particular industry or geography.

## Reduce recurring manual work

- In Integrations, enable scheduled Gmail checks when ready. Optionally enable automatic reply drafts. Clear interest/meeting requests are drafted into Approvals; pricing, complaints and ambiguous replies require a person. A repeated inbox import does not create another draft.
- In Sequences, create a bounded follow-up schedule and enrol reviewed contacts. Follow-up drafts enter Approvals. Replies, opt-outs, meetings and conversions stop the sequence. The current beta requires review before sending each draft.
- In Campaigns, freeze a reviewed recipient batch and approve it once. Connected bulk sending requires recorded opt-in for every recipient and still enforces suppression and caps.
- Verified payment receipts update customers, subscriptions and onboarding. A checkout URL or successful browser redirect does not mark a customer paid.
- In Content, prepare reviewed WordPress.com/Jetpack articles or LinkedIn member text posts. Test in sandbox first. An uncertain publication requires provider-console review and is never blindly repeated. Company-page posts and other social networks are not yet supported.
- Attribution connects recorded campaign interactions with gross succeeded payments using first-touch, last-touch or equal credit across distinct campaigns. Record campaign expenses with evidence. Unknown/unassigned value stays visible; the current attribution report does not allocate all model costs or refunds.

## Current limit

The goal is minimal setup and extensive routine automation. **99% autonomy has not been measured or achieved.** External sends/publication/bookings retain exact approvals; sensitive commercial decisions remain with the owner. Paid-ad execution, raster-image generation, richer per-role quality validation and production hardening still need work. Credentials alone do not complete those features or guarantee clients, revenue, rankings or deliverability.
