# Running your agency workflow

1. Create your account and workspace. For an independent client, use a separate workspace and invite only the people who need access. Roles are owner, admin, member and viewer.
2. Add a Brand Brain profile for your agency or product: offer, audience, geography, voice, prohibited claims and supported facts. Enter prices only after deciding them. Upload source text/PDFs or read a public website in Knowledge. Imported text is evidence, not an instruction for agents.
3. In Command Center, describe one narrow objective and audience. Review the generated plan and missing information. Approve internal work, then inspect outputs in Plans & tasks and Content.
4. Build your own service page from approved content. Request publication approval, inspect the frozen payload, approve, and choose **Publish on this server**. Its public link appears in Content. Localhost pages are accessible only on your computer until you configure hosting. Enquiries go to the private CRM/inbox and do not establish marketing permission.
5. Add prospects using sourced contact entry/CSV, connected Brave website research, or **Read a public business contact page**. Research results are suggestions. Enrichment imports only observed email addresses matching that business domain. It never guesses email addresses. Review evidence and contactability before outreach.
6. Review the CRM contact. Add a short, sourced observation if useful. Editing contact facts invalidates existing approvals. Score the contact and create a draft. Review the exact text, citations and opt-out; revise supported facts in Brand Brain when needed.
7. Request and approve the draft. First execute in sandbox. After provider setup and a small real test, choose connected execution for an approved recipient. Bulk batches use Resend and require recorded consent for each recipient, owner approval, a verified sender domain and a daily cap. Follow-ups draft new messages; they do not bypass approval.
8. Review Inbox, including website enquiries and scheduled Gmail sync. Ambiguous, pricing, legal, refund and dispute requests need human attention. Unsubscribe/bounce/complaint suppression blocks later sends. No heuristic confidence score is a calibrated quality guarantee.
9. Propose a meeting and approve its exact details. Calendar checks availability before booking. An external user can still occupy the slot concurrently; confirm unusual conflicts manually.
10. Create a Checkout using your approved price. A verified matching payment receipt creates the customer and onboarding work. A browser redirect never marks paid. Subscription renewals/failures/cancellations and refunds/disputes update from signed provider events; the app does not initiate refunds or disputes.
11. Review customer onboarding, support, internal automation, expenses and analytics. Record actual search/sending/infrastructure/review expenses so cost ratios reflect your business. Sandbox figures remain separate from real receipts.
12. Daily: review failed/blocked tasks, pending approvals, uncertain external receipts, unread notifications and held model costs. Use emergency stop to block new work when investigating. It cannot recall an external request already in flight. Back up privately and practice restore.

Do not expect a plan alone to guarantee clients. Your offer, evidence, audience, deliverability and human follow-up determine results. Begin with a small reviewed campaign and improve from recorded responses. Broad social-network publishing, paid-ad management and AI-generated raster images are not included in this release.
